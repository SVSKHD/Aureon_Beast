# Aureon V4 Gold — TODO 061-070

This block turns the V4 journey data into specialist and historical-evidence intelligence. It remains research-only and does not place trades.

## 061 — Remaining-move specialist

The existing V4 remaining-move learner is promoted to an explicit specialist contract rather than duplicated. It predicts the monotonic +3/+5/+10/+20/+30/+40 ladder plus expected remaining MFE and MAE.

## 062 — Pullback specialist

Resolved V4 re-entry observations train a separate continuation-after-pullback model. Inputs include pullback depth/fraction/class, ATR, structure/EMA alignment, retests, and phase-volume behaviour.

## 063 — Exhaustion specialist

A dedicated binary specialist estimates the probability that less than +3 remains from an observable anchor. This is evidence about movement exhaustion, not an instruction to exit or reverse.

## 064 — MAE specialist

Expected remaining adverse excursion is exposed through a dedicated specialist contract so risk research can consume it independently from directional probabilities.

## 065 — Regime router

Specialists are trained only in session + phase + HTF-alignment + trend cells with enough resolved samples. Sparse cells explicitly fall back instead of publishing unstable regime models.

## 066-068 — Similar historical journeys

Every resolved V4 row gets a deterministic embedding from frozen V4 numeric/categorical features. Retrieval:
- excludes the query journey itself,
- defaults to the same symbol,
- normalizes numeric feature ranges,
- penalizes categorical, symbol and direction mismatch,
- returns deterministic nearest journeys.

The outcome summary reports sample count, mean similarity, +3/+5/+10 rates, and mean remaining MFE/MAE.

## 069-070 — Positive and negative evidence

The explanation layer keeps supporting and opposing evidence separate. It reports factual observations such as historical hit rates, frozen HTF alignment and movement already consumed. These are evidence statements, not causal explanations.
