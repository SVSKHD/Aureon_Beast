# Aureon V4 Gold — TODO 041-060

This slice extends V4 from pullback tracking into session, higher-timeframe and
state-transition intelligence.

## 041-042 — phase volume

V4 now stores:
- pullback mean tick volume and whether it contracted relative to expansion,
- continuation mean tick volume and whether participation expanded again.

These remain relative MT5 tick-volume observations, not centralized exchange volume.

## 043-050 — independent session intelligence

Asia, London and New York are evaluated independently from the configured session
windows. A candle may belong to both London and New York during overlap.

The new session tracker records:
- session open context,
- running high/low/close,
- EMA20/50 and EMA200 cross counts,
- journeys observed,
- session close outcome.

Research helpers produce session and session-phase outcome summaries and train
separate continuation specialists only where minimum sample support is met.

## 051-053 — M15/H1 context

V4 can freeze M15 and H1 EMA/bias reads from the already-observable MTF context at
an M5 anchor. The context is descriptive; it creates no additional Discord alert.

An HTF effect report compares continuation and remaining-movement outcomes by the
frozen alignment state.

## 054-059 — state transitions

Completed journeys can be converted into explicit transition evidence:

- pre-cross -> confirmed cross,
- confirmed cross -> expansion,
- expansion -> pullback,
- pullback -> continuation,
- expansion -> exhaustion.

Negative transition rows are preserved, so a transition that failed to occur is
still part of the dataset.

## 060 — continuation specialist

A dedicated short-continuation specialist predicts +3, +5 and +10 from V4 movement
features. Its probability ladder is constrained so larger targets can never be more
probable than smaller targets.

This remains research intelligence. No order placement or automatic trade action is
introduced.
