# Aureon V4 Gold — TODO 041-050

This slice completes the V4 volume-transition and session-intelligence block.

## TODO 041-042 — pullback and continuation volume

V4 keeps phase volume separate:

- expansion tick-volume mean,
- pullback tick-volume mean and whether it contracted versus expansion,
- continuation tick-volume mean and whether it re-expanded versus the pullback/expansion baseline.

These are relative MT5 tick-volume observations, not centralized exchange volume.

## TODO 043-046 — independent sessions

Asia, London and New York are evaluated independently from the configured market-time
windows. A candle may therefore belong to both London and New York.

The explicit overlap label is:

`london_new_york`

This does not change the legacy exclusive `session_for()` contract used by older V1-V3
records. V4 gets a new independent context so historical contracts are not silently
reinterpreted.

## TODO 047 — session open context

At a true configured session boundary V4 freezes:

- session open price,
- EMA20 / EMA50 / EMA200,
- current trend label,
- previous completed session,
- previous session change and range.

Starting Aureon in the middle of a session does not fabricate an open snapshot.

## TODO 048 — session close outcome

Each independently tracked session records:

- open / high / low / close,
- net change and range,
- candle count,
- EMA20/50 cross count,
- price/EMA200 cross count,
- journey-start count.

London can close while New York remains active.

## TODO 049-050 — session-wise learning

Resolved V4 movement rows can be grouped into independent specialist cells:

- Asia early / mid / late,
- London early / mid / late,
- New York early / mid / late,
- London-New York overlap.

Cells below `min_samples` remain explicitly insufficient. Sufficient cells expose
+3/+5/+10 continuation rates, mean MFE/MAE and mean pullback depth. This is a research
layer for later model routing; it does not execute trades or bypass model governance.
